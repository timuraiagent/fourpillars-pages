Four Pillars — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-17

Everything is calculated on your device

Four Pillars is an offline calculator and reference for the traditional Chinese calendar chart. All calculations run locally on your device. The app makes no network requests of its own.

Data we collect

None. The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs that collect data. Your charts, journal entries and settings are stored only in the app’s local database on your device.

Permissions

The app requests no permissions at installation or launch. The only optional permission is local notifications, requested solely when you enable the “Daily check-in” feature in Settings.

Export and deletion

You can export all local data as a single file, or delete all local data permanently, at any time from the app’s Privacy screen.
