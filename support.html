Four Pillars — Support

What this app is

An offline calculator and reference for the traditional Chinese Four Pillars (八字) calendar chart. It describes the structure of a chart and calendar cycles; it does not make claims about outcomes.

How to test

Tap “Explore a sample chart” on the first screen, or enter a date, time and birthplace manually. Everything works in airplane mode.
